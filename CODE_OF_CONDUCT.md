# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

The Developer Lens Lab community is intended to be welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free.
Participants should make room for different backgrounds, abilities, identities, experience levels,
and viewpoints while keeping research discussion constructive and evidence-bounded.

## Expected behaviour

Examples of behaviour that contributes to a healthy community include:

- treating people with empathy and respect;
- discussing methods, evidence, and trade-offs without personal attacks;
- accepting constructive feedback and taking responsibility for mistakes; and
- respecting boundaries, consent, and the privacy of others.

Unacceptable behaviour includes harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualised attention, doxxing,
publishing another person's private information, impersonation, and conduct that would not be
appropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope and moderation

This Code applies in Lab issues, pull requests, Discussions, reviews, and other spaces when someone
is representing the Lab. Maintainers may edit, remove, close, or restrict participation when needed
to keep those spaces safe and on topic. Research disagreement is welcome; harassment is not.

## Requesting a private conduct channel

The Lab currently has **no dedicated conduct inbox, no continuous monitoring service, and no published
contact address**. Do not put incident details in a public issue. Do not post identities, screenshots,
logs, links, credentials, or other private data.

Opening any issue publicly reveals the GitHub account that opened it. If that visibility is unsafe,
do not submit a request. Otherwise, to request contact without publicly revealing conduct-reporting
intent, open the Lab's neutral
[private contact request template](.github/ISSUE_TEMPLATE/private_contact_request.md) and put only
this content in the issue body:

> Please arrange a private contact channel.

That content-free request is only a way to ask for contact; it is not a place to report an incident.
The maintainer can then provide the next step when available.
