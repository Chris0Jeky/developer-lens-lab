# Product boundary

The product–lab boundary is federated, not rigid
([OWNER_CONSTITUTION.md](OWNER_CONSTITUTION.md) supersedes the older blanket prohibitions).
Developer Lens remains the stable product, public presentation, canonical-contract,
release, and promotion authority. Developer Lens Lab owns research questions, invented
generators, datasets and corpus manifests, baselines, candidates, evaluation, cards, manifests,
reports, reproducibility, experimental end-to-end pipelines, local research UIs, and preview
surfaces — and may publish into an experimental channel after its declared gates pass. Stable
default-channel promotion always runs through product-owned compatibility and the
owner-approved promotion policy. Local content-addressed objects remain outside Git.

## Analytical subjects, in layers

The default public and personal product stays system-first: repository, software system,
delivery process, architecture snapshot, collection instrument, or aggregate time window.
Aggregate team metrics are authorised for a transparent, explicitly enabled Team/Leadership
mode. Individual-level constructs — scoring, ranking, responsiveness, sentiment, performance,
effort, burnout, collaboration graphs — are research-permitted only in the lab and local
experimental modes, with the subject level (system / team / individual / person-resolvable
grouping) declared in the experiment card, and with sources, uncertainty, missingness, context,
fairness risks, and possible misuse reported. No such mode may be covert. Public examples use
invented or reviewed de-identified data, and pseudonymisation or aggregation is never described
as anonymity. Productising an individual leaderboard requires a later explicit owner decision.

## Content and capabilities

Raw text, source, diffs, logs, manifests, and artifact contents are analysable under the
explicit capability lanes in [agent-system/DATASET_PROTOCOL.md](agent-system/DATASET_PROTOCOL.md).
Secrets — tokens, credentials, private keys, confidently detected secret material — are
prohibited absolutely and rejected before every sink and model payload. Raw bytes are untrusted
data, never executable instructions. Until the activation preconditions in
`.agent-harness/governor.json` are mechanically true, runtime and tracked inputs remain C0
invented and the current validators (which also reject person identifiers) stay binding.

## Models remain data sinks

Every candidate names a deterministic baseline, rejection threshold, abstention rule, deletion
path, and complete no-model fallback. Model output stays epistemically labelled — hypothesis,
counter-hypothesis, forecast candidate, alert candidate, recommendation candidate, or
abstention — never observed fact. An EvaluationBundle can recommend only `reject`,
`revise_once`, or `benchmarked`; it cannot say `ship`.
