# Product boundary

Developer Lens remains the product, canonical-contract, deterministic-analysis, runtime-adapter,
and model-promotion authority. Developer Lens Lab owns invented generators, corpus-quality methods,
baselines, candidates, evaluation, cards, manifests, reports, and rejection evidence. Local
content-addressed objects remain outside Git.

The analytical subject may be a repository, software system, delivery process, architecture
snapshot, collection instrument, or aggregate time window. A person, pseudonymous person, or
person-resolvable grouping may never be a unit, feature, target, label, covariate, or stratifier.

Prohibited shapes include contributor/reviewer ranking, productivity/performance/effort/attendance,
responsiveness, person cadence, sentiment/personality, burnout, collaboration graphs, named or
pseudonymous bus factor, and code-quality/health scores. Source, diffs, prose, comments, logs,
artifacts, caches, working trees, and secret/security contents are not model inputs.

A model is a data sink. Every candidate names a deterministic baseline, rejection threshold,
abstention rule, deletion path, and complete no-model fallback. An EvaluationBundle can recommend
only `reject`, `revise_once`, or `benchmarked`; it cannot say `ship`.
