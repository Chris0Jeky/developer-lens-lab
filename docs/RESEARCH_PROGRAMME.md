# Research programme

The first vertical is WB-C1: an invented weekly system-series benchmark. It must include no-change
negative controls; level, variance, slope, and seasonal-amplitude changes; Gaussian and heavy-tailed
noise; missing blocks; and coverage/permission/parser-shift confounders with no system change.

The deterministic rolling median/MAD alerter and online candidate receive symmetric nested
threshold selection. Disjoint repository/seed-family panels and non-overlapping time windows prevent
cross-partition leakage; this first vertical does not claim rolling-origin evaluation. The primary
online metric is false alerts per year at a fixed detection-delay budget. PELT is offline descriptive
only and receives a boundary/localisation summary, never an invented online delay. The final holdout
opens once through an explicit custody event.

The smoke design uses two train seed families: one inner-fit family and one inner-validation family.
A threshold is viable only when it meets the fixed detection and delay floor on both; selection then
uses the inner-validation false-alert objective. Test and final-holdout families cannot influence
that choice. False-alert exposure and its numerator both exclude the fixed nine-week event window
(change week plus the eight-week delay budget). Incomplete series are ineligible for the PELT arm;
they are never interpolated into a complete offline history.

An invented result proves mechanics, privacy, negative controls, failure handling, removability,
and planted-effect recovery only. It cannot establish real validity. The initial decision may be
rejection; the deterministic fallback remains complete.

Later candidates and every real dataset are parked behind evidence and owner gates. Public corpus
work begins with a quality/support report, never training. WB-C6 or WB-C7 may be considered only
after a separately approved representative dataset exists.
